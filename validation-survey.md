## How to Use This Survey:

1. **Post on Reddit**: r/startups, r/entrepreneur, r/financialindependence
2. **LinkedIn**: Share with your professional network
3. **Twitter/X**: Use relevant hashtags like #news #AI #productivity
4. **Industry-specific forums**: Find where your target audience hangs out
5. **Cold outreach**: Email 50-100 professionals in your target industry

**Success Metrics:**
- Aim for 100+ responses
- Look for 60%+ saying they'd pay for the solution
- Identify the most common pain points
- Find the sweet spot for pricing
